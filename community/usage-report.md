# Usage report

A usage report records one real use of a skill. It's the evidence that moves a skill from `draft` to `tested-elsewhere`, and it tells the next educator what to expect. Submit it through the **Usage report** issue form or the web form on the catalog site.

Don't include patient data, learner names or grades, or unpublished material.

## Questions

1. **Skill and version** (from `card.yaml`)
2. **Your institution** and your role (course leader, facilitator, coordinator, other)
3. **AI tool and plan** (e.g. ChatGPT Edu, Claude Pro, Gemini Enterprise, Copilot Cowork, paste-in version in a free plan)
4. **Task** — what you asked it to do, in one or two sentences
5. **Outcome** — used as-is / used after edits / not used
6. **Time** — roughly how long until you had a usable result
7. **Help needed** — none / documentation / help from a colleague / help from the skill's author
8. **What worked**
9. **What didn't** — be specific: a step it skipped, a wrong assumption, a format problem
10. **What you changed** — edits to the output, or to the skill itself (if you adapted the skill, please share the change)
11. **Would you use it again?** yes / maybe / no — why
12. **May we list your institution** in the skill's `used_at`? yes / no

## What maintainers do with it

- Link it in the skill card (`used_at`), update `tested_in` for that tool, and update `maturity` if the evidence now supports it.
- Turn "what didn't" into issues, and "what you changed" into improvements — with credit.
