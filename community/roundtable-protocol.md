# Round-table protocol (workshop)

For hands-on workshops where participants bring a real task from their institution and work on it with the library's skills at small tables. The protocol doubles as a test of the library: can someone who didn't build a skill use it, adapt it, and want to keep using it?

## Before the workshop

- **Ask participants to bring one real task** (anonymised): a session to design, objectives to fix, a paper to turn into a reading, a syllabus to update, exam items to write. No patient data, no learner data.
- **Ask which AI tool and plan they have.** Prepare the matching install path for each (ZIP upload, plugin, paste-in version). Have the paste-in versions printed or on a USB stick for anyone whose tool lacks skill support.
- **One facilitator per 2–3 tables**, who knows the skills but doesn't do the task for participants.
- **Print one recording sheet per participant** (below), or use the web form.

## Timing (75-minute session)

| Time | Segment |
|---|---|
| 10 min | Why shared skills; how a skill works; the objectives table that links the skills |
| 5 min | Choose a table by task (objectives / sessions / readings / syllabus / exam items) |
| 10 min | Install or load the skill in your own tool; facilitators help |
| 30 min | Work on your own task with the skill |
| 10 min | Table debrief: what worked, what you changed, what you'd change in the skill |
| 10 min | Whole-room harvest; how to contribute; next steps |

## Recording sheet (one per participant)

1. Table / skill:
2. Institution, role:
3. Tool and plan:
4. Task (one sentence):
5. Minutes until a first usable output: ___
6. Help needed: none / facilitator once / facilitator several times / couldn't proceed
7. What you changed in the output:
8. What you'd change in the skill:
9. Would you use it again after today? yes / maybe / no
10. Willing to test it at your institution and send a usage report? yes / no — email (optional):

## After the workshop

- Enter sheets as usage reports (with consent). Update `tested_in` per tool.
- Open issues for "what you'd change in the skill".
- Invite participants who said yes to Q10 to the next community call.

## What we measure

The headline number is not attendance but **independent reuse**: the share of participants who reached a usable output with at most one facilitator intervention, and the number who later return a usage report or an improvement.
