# Pedagogical basis

Why this skill does what it does. Labels: Standard (NIH announcement, notice or application guide), Practice (how experienced program teams prepare training grants; programs may change it).

| Rule in this skill | Basis | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Confirm the current announcement and notices before every use | Standard | PAR-24-128 banner of March 31, 2025 and Related Notices; NIH Guide for Grants and Contracts |
| Components, headings, page limits, file names and review questions | Standard | PAR-24-128, Sections IV and V; SF424 (R&R) Application Guide, Training instructions |
| MSTP-AHeAD program area and expenses | Standard | NOT-GM-25-024 |
| Training table changes and no Table 6A | Standard | NOT-GM-24-053; NIH Updates to Institutional Training Grant Applications |
| Biosketches in SciENcv Common Forms | Standard | NOT-OD-26-018 |
| NRSA stipend, tuition and expense levels | Standard | NOT-OD-26-044 |
| The program team writes the narrative | Standard | NOT-OD-25-132 |
| Trainee data stays aggregate or de-identified in AI tools | Standard | Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act; institutional data policies |
| Backward timeline and internal mock review | Practice | Experience of training grant directors and sponsored programs offices |
