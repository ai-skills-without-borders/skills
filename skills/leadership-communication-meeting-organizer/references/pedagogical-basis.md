# Pedagogical basis

Why this skill does what it does. Labels: Research (published studies), Standard (a widely used published guideline), Practice (how experienced leaders run speaking practice; programs may change it).

| Rule in this skill | Basis | Source |
|---|---|---|
| People improve by speaking repeatedly with immediate, specific feedback | Research | Ericsson, 2004 |
| Feedback names a specific strength and a specific next step, about the task, not the person | Research | Hattie and Timperley, 2007 |
| Feedback is timely, specific, given in a safe setting and ends with a plan | Standard | Ramani and Krackov, 2012 |
| Low-stakes setting, ground rules and normalizing nerves | Practice | Leaders of speaking practice groups |
| Rotating listening and speaking roles so everyone takes part | Practice | Structured speaking practice meetings |
| Prompts avoid private or sensitive topics and anyone can pass | Practice | Educators running group sessions |
