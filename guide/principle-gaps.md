## 2. Principles that no skill applies yet

Candidates for a new skill, or an addition to an existing one.

- **Spaced practice** (science). Spreading practice over time beats massing it, and the gap should grow with how long knowledge must last.
- **Deliberate practice and simulation** (medicine). Expertise grows through focused practice on specific weaknesses with immediate feedback, not through experience alone.
- **Professional identity formation** (medicine). Medical education should help learners come to think, act and feel like physicians (and, for MD-PhD students, like physician-scientists), not only perform competently.
- **AI that protects learning** (ai). Unrestricted AI help can raise practice scores and lower real learning. Student-facing AI should guide rather than hand over answers, and its effects should be studied.
