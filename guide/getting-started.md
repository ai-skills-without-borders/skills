# New to skills? Start here

No coding and no AI expertise needed. If you can describe how you teach, you can use a skill, and eventually write one.

## What is a skill?

**A skill is transferable teaching know-how, packaged for an AI assistant.** It is a short set of written instructions that an educator wrote once, so that anyone's AI assistant can do one teaching task the way an experienced colleague would.

Think of it as a protocol for the AI. A good protocol holds two things:

- **Knowledge:** the standards, rules and common mistakes an expert knows. For example, "one objective, one target" or "no 'all of the above' in exam items".
- **A framework:** the steps in order, the questions to ask first, the checkpoints where you approve, and the exact shape of the result.

A skill holds both, and that is what makes it transferable: across people, schools, countries, AI tools, and time.

You don't open a skill or run it. Once it is installed, you ask your assistant in ordinary words, for example *"write exam questions for my renal objectives"*. The assistant recognizes the task, loads the right skill, and follows it.

## What's inside a skill

| Part | Where | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **When to use it** | The description at the top of `SKILL.md` | Lists the words educators actually say, so the assistant knows when to pick the skill up |
| **The framework** | The body of `SKILL.md` | Sets the steps, the questions to ask you, where to stop for your approval, and what the result looks like |
| **The knowledge** | The `references/` folder | Holds standards, checklists, examples and rules, loaded only when a step needs them |

## A prompt or a skill?

| A one-off prompt | A skill |
|---|---|
| Written from scratch each time, by each person | Written once by someone who knows the task well |
| Quality depends on how well you prompted today | Same standards and steps every time, for everyone |
| Lost when the chat ends | Installed once, available in every chat |
| Hard to share, review or improve | Shared, tested at other schools, and improved over time |

## How to use one

1. **Install it.** Download the skill from the [catalog](https://ai-skills-without-borders.github.io/skills/) and upload it in your AI tool's skills settings ([install guide](install.md)). No skills in your plan? Use the paste-in version.
2. **Ask in plain words.** Describe your task and attach your material: slides, a paper, your objectives. The skill asks for anything it still needs.
3. **Review and decide.** The skill stops for your approval at key points, flags what it couldn't verify, and never invents sources. You stay the expert.

## Why it's worth it

- **Expertise travels.** What one experienced educator knows about writing objectives or exam items reaches colleagues in other schools and countries, without a workshop.
- **Consistent quality.** Every course leader gets the same checks: one target per objective, no flawed exam items, timings that add up.
- **Less time on the mechanics.** The routine parts get done quickly, leaving more of your time for teaching decisions.
- **Not locked to one tool.** Skills use an open format, so your institution can switch AI providers without losing the work.

## Common questions

**Is a skill just a long prompt?**
Partly. Unlike a prompt, it is saved and loaded automatically when it fits, it can carry reference files and examples, and it is versioned, tested and shared like any other teaching resource.

**Does a skill send my material anywhere?**
No. A skill is only instructions. Your material goes wherever your AI tool already sends it, so follow your institution's rules for that tool, and never share identifiable patient or student data.

**Do I need to know how to code?**
No. Only one skill here has optional scripts, and it still works without them. Writing a new skill means writing clear instructions in plain English.

**Can I change a skill for my school?**
Yes. Skills are openly licensed (CC BY 4.0). Adapt the wording, your framework or your templates, and consider [sharing the improvement back](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
