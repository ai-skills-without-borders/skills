Put your institution on the map

The library is at its start: the map grows with every institution that joins. Every contribution to a skill counts, from a five-minute usage report to a new skill.

  • Every contribution names an institution and a country. The forms ask for them, and pull requests say them in the description.
  • Your name appears only if you agree. Tick "credit me by name" in the form; otherwise only your institution is shown.
  • Counted when accepted. A maintainer adds the contribution to the community record when it is merged or received.
