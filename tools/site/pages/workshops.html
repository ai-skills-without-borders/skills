Workshops

Two hands-on workshops that anyone can lead, at any institution. Each comes with a full lesson plan, timings, a script, slides to present and a PowerPoint to adapt, handouts, and step-by-step instructions for participants who have never done this before.

Which one first? Start with Using AI skills if most participants have never installed a skill. Run Writing AI skills for educators who have used at least one skill, or as the follow-up a few weeks later.

What each workshop includes

  • PreparationA checklist for the facilitator from three weeks before, the pre-work for participants, and an invitation email to copy.
  • Timing and scriptEvery part with its minutes, what you do, what participants do, what to say, and how you know the room is ready to move on.
  • SlidesPresent them in the browser, with speaker notes, or download the PowerPoint to translate or adapt.
  • HandoutsStep-by-step guides written for first-timers: installing a skill in each AI tool, opening a GitHub account, submitting a skill.
  • When things go wrongThe problems that come up in real sessions, and what to do about each.

Adapting a workshop for your institution or language? The materials are openly licensed (CC BY 4.0). We would love to hear how it went: write to us, or suggest a change.
