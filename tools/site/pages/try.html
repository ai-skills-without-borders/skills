Try your first skill

One example, in the AI assistant you already use. Nothing to install, and no account on this site. You will see a skill check an exam question for item-writing flaws, and learn how to judge what it gives you.

  1. Open a new chat in your AI assistant

    Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or Copilot, on any plan, free plans included. If your institution provides one, use it and follow its rules.

  2. Copy the skill and the example

    One button copies everything the assistant needs: the Exam Question Writer skill, the example question below, and a request to review it.

    The example question (made up for practice){{QUESTION}}

    The copy already tells the assistant what it would otherwise ask you: who the question is for and which objective it tests. With your own material, the skill asks you instead.

  3. Paste it into the chat and send

    It is a long message: the skill's instructions come first, and your request is at the end. That is expected. The run happens in your assistant, not on this site.

  4. Check what comes back

    Outputs differ between runs and tools, so look for these signs rather than for the same words:

    • It names the flaws it found, against a checklist: the negative stem, "All of the above", the longest option being the answer, the options in mixed forms.
    • It keeps what the question tests (the kidney's functions) instead of swapping in a new question.
    • It doesn't claim the answer is checked: with no source given, the keyed answer is marked unverified.
    • It tells you what still needs your judgement, for example a lead-in that can't be answered with the options covered.
