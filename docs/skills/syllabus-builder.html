Syllabus Builder

Builds or updates a syllabus from partial course material, with an evidence-based (E/P/X) map to any competency framework.

MaturityDraft
Version0.1.0
Runs codeNo
Maintained byAmit Shraga, PhD (Weizmann Institute of Science)

When to use it

Builds or updates a course syllabus from whatever course material exists (complete or partial) and maps the course to the institution's competency framework with evidence-based rules — institutional EPAs, AAMC Foundational Competencies, the AAMC GREAT MD-PhD competencies, AI competencies, or any framework the educator supplies. For course leaders in medical, MD-PhD, PhD and biomedical-research programs, any course kind (block, module, longitudinal track, lab course, elective) and any teaching format (lecture, PBL, RBL, TBL, CBL, simulation, labs, journal club). Use whenever someone wants to create, draft, fill in, update or review a syllabus or course outline; map a course or assignment to competencies, EPAs or milestones; check which competencies a course covers or where its gaps are; or says "here are my course materials, make the syllabus" — even with only an outline, a few slide decks or a verbal description.

Use it to

  • Update a syllabus
  • Map a course to competencies or EPAs

Pedagogical basis

What each rule rests on. Research Standard Practice — rules labelled Practice are local conventions your institution may change. All principles and references.

Rule in this skill Basis Source
Objectives, schedule and assessment linked Standard Constructive alignment — Biggs, 1996, Higher Education 32:347–364
Competency counts as evidence only where performed and observed/assessed Standard Entrustment — ten Cate, 2005, Medical Education 39:1176–1177; validity of assessment for its use — Downing, 2003, Medical Education 37:830–837
Framework wording only from the supplied document; map is a proposal Standard AAMC/AACOM/ACGME Foundational Competencies (2024): formal faculty review required; GREAT MD-PhD competencies — Estrada et al., 2026, Academic Medicine
E / P / X levels; re-check above six E links; "cheap upgrades" Practice Local method against inflated maps; P → E by adding observation or a rubric follows the entrustment logic
Nothing factual invented; visible placeholders Standard AAMC AI Principles (2025): transparency; a syllabus is a contract with learners
