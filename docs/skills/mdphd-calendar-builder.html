MD-PhD Calendar Builder

Helps MD-PhD program directors and administrators build one complete calendar for their program.

MaturityDraft
Version0.1.0
Used byProgram staff
Runs codeNo
Maintained byTalia H. Swartz, MD, PhD (Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai)

When to use it

Helps MD-PhD program directors and administrators build a complete program calendar for any MD-PhD program. Use when someone says "help me build our program calendar", "make a master calendar for the MD-PhD program", "what dates should our program track", "map our students' milestones by year", "when should we send committee meeting reminders" or "put our events and deadlines in one place". It covers the yearly program cycle, each student's milestones from admission to residency, national dates such as AMCAS, NIH fellowships and the Match, and reminders with enough lead time. It works from the program's own structure and rules and never invents dates.

Use it to

  • Help me build our program calendar
  • Make a master calendar for the MD-PhD program
  • What dates should our program track
  • Map our students' milestones by year
  • When should we send committee meeting reminders
  • Put our events and deadlines in one place

You bring

  • The program's structure
  • The program's rules for lab choice, qualifying exam, committee meetings and return to clinic
  • Entering years of current cohorts
  • Existing calendars, handbooks and the medical school and graduate school academic calendars
  • Where the calendar will live, such as Google Calendar, Outlook or a shared spreadsheet
  • Any grants with reporting dates, such as a T32
the skill

You get

  • A master calendar table with event, date or window, audience, cohort, owner, reminders, source and status
  • Views by month, by cohort and by owner
  • A list of clashes with exams, holidays and religious observances
  • A CSV file to import confirmed dates into Google Calendar or Outlook
  • An upkeep plan naming who refreshes the calendar each year

Pedagogical basis

What each rule rests on. Research Standard Practice — rules labelled Practice are local conventions your institution may change. All principles and references.

Rule in this skill Basis Source
Track milestones through both degrees because time to degree has grown Research Brass and Akabas, 2019
Regular mentor and committee meetings and yearly development plans Research National Academies of Sciences, Engineering, and Medicine, 2019
Yearly Individual Development Plans for trainees Standard NIH NOT-OD-14-113
Admissions acceptance dates Standard AAMC Application and Acceptance Protocols for Applicants
NIH fellowship and training grant due dates Standard NIH standard due dates and current announcements
Never invent dates; name the source and year checked Practice Program teams
Owners by role, lead times and the yearly refresh Practice Program teams
Keep individual progress out of shared calendars Standard Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act
