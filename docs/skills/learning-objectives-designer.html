Learning Objectives Designer

Extracts, writes, splits, levels and maps learning objectives; produces the numbered objectives table the other skills use.

MaturityDraft
Version0.1.0
Runs codeNo
Maintained byAmit Shraga, PhD (Weizmann Institute of Science)

When to use it

Extracts, writes, simplifies, levels and maps learning objectives for a course, module, session or assignment in medical or biomedical-research education. Use whenever an educator wants to write or fix learning objectives, pull objectives out of existing course material (slides, syllabi, session plans, notebooks), split compound objectives into single focused ones, assign cognitive levels (Bloom or a simpler three-level scale), check alignment between objectives, activities and assessment, or map objectives to a competency framework (institutional EPAs, AAMC Foundational Competencies, GREAT MD-PhD competencies, AI competencies). Also use when asked "what are the objectives of this course", "are my objectives measurable", "map my course", or before generating exam questions or a syllabus. Produces a numbered objectives table that the syllabus-builder and exam-question-writer skills consume.

Use it to

  • Write learning objectives
  • Fix vague or compound objectives
  • Map a course to competencies
  • Check alignment of objectives, activities and assessment

You bring

  • Course material (slides, syllabi, notebooks) or a description
  • Optional: competency framework document
the skill

You get

  • Alignment gaps
  • Proposed framework links

Pedagogical basis

What each rule rests on. Research Standard Practice — rules labelled Practice are local conventions your institution may change. All principles and references.

Rule in this skill Basis Source
Check every objective for an activity and an assessment Standard Constructive alignment — Biggs, 1996, Higher Education 32:347–364
One observable verb; level matched to the activity Standard Revised Bloom taxonomy — Anderson & Krathwohl, 2001
Condition and criterion for skills and procedures Standard Mager, 1962, Preparing Objectives for Programmed Instruction
One target per objective Research + Practice Cognitive load — Sweller, 1988, Cognitive Science 12:257–285; also makes alignment and item writing checkable
Framework links are proposals for faculty review Standard AAMC/AACOM/ACGME Foundational Competencies (2024): no external framework without formal faculty review
E / P / X strength of a framework link Practice Applies entrustment (performed and observed) — ten Cate, 2005, Medical Education 39:1176–1177
Three levels instead of six Practice Easier faculty agreement; Bloom level still recorded
AI-assistance line; no learner data Standard AAMC Principles for the Responsible Use of AI in Medical Education (2025)
