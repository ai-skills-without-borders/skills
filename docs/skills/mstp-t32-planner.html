The description the AI reads

Helps MD-PhD program directors and administrators pull together everything needed for an NIGMS Medical Scientist Training Program (MSTP) T32 application, new or renewal. Use when someone says "we're putting together our MSTP T32", "help me plan our training grant renewal", "what do we need for the MSTP T32", "make a checklist for the T32", "what goes in the organizational support letter", "help me collect the training tables" or "check our T32 against the announcement". It first checks that the team is using the current NIH announcement and notices, then builds a component tracker, a timeline, data and letter requests, an outline of the Program Plan mapped to the review criteria and a final compliance check. The program leaders write the application.

Why you can trust it

Every rule in this skill is labelled with what it rests on: Research Standard Practice. Rules labelled Practice are local conventions your institution may change.
