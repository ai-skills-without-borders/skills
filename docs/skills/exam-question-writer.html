Exam Question Writer

Blueprints and writes objective-aligned SBA, short-answer and data-interpretation items, checked for item-writing flaws.

When to use it

Writes exam and practice questions aligned to learning objectives for medical and biomedical-research education — single-best-answer MCQs with clinical or research vignettes, short-answer items with rubrics, and data-interpretation items — starting from a blueprint and checking every item for known item-writing flaws. Use whenever an educator wants exam questions, quiz items, practice questions, a question bank, an assessment blueprint, or wants existing items reviewed, improved, tagged to objectives, or exported for an LMS (Moodle GIFT, CSV). Works best with the numbered objectives table from the learning-objectives-designer skill; with only a topic or material, it first drafts focused objectives. Asks whether items are for formative practice or summative assessment and adjusts rigour accordingly.

Pedagogical basis

What each rule rests on. Research Standard Practice — rules labelled Practice are local conventions your institution may change. All principles and references.

Rule in this skill Basis Source
Blueprint first; one item tests one objective at its level Standard Constructive alignment — Biggs, 1996; levels — Anderson & Krathwohl, 2001
Flaw checklist; cover-the-options; homogeneous options; no "all/none of the above" Standard + Research 31 validated guidelines — Haladyna, Downing & Rodriguez, 2002, Applied Measurement in Education 15:309–333; NBME Item-Writing Guide, 6th ed. (2020)
Flaws are fixed, not tolerated Research 36–65% of items in four medical-school exams were flawed; flawed items disadvantaged some students — Downing, 2005, Advances in Health Sciences Education 10:133–143
Ask formative or summative; summative needs expert review, piloting and item analysis Standard Validity for intended use — Downing, 2003, Medical Education 37:830–837; assessment utility — van der Vleuten, 1996, Advances in Health Sciences Education 1:41–67; AAMC AI facilitator guide (2025)
Rationale for every option in formative use Research Retrieval practice with feedback — Roediger & Karpicke, 2006; Larsen, Butler & Roediger, 2008
Fair vignettes; no demographic cueing Standard NBME Item-Writing Guide (2020): patient characteristics guidance
AI-written items are drafts Standard AAMC AI Principles (2025)
