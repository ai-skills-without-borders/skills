Part 1: What a skill is

A one-off prompt and a skill

A one-off prompt

  • Written from scratch each time, by each person
  • Quality depends on how well you prompted today
  • Lost when the chat ends
  • Hard to share, review or improve

A skill

  • Written once by someone who knows the task well
  • Same standards and steps every time, for everyone
  • Installed once, available in every chat
  • Shared, tested at other institutions, improved over time

What happens behind the scenes

  1. You ask in plain words, with your material attached

Review before you use it

  • Correct? Facts, numbers, dates, terms against your source
  • Invented anything? References, page numbers, policies
  • Fits? Your learners, your course, your rules
  • As promised? Compare with the skill page's expected output
  • What would you change? Write it down
